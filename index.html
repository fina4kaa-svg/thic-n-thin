Thic n Thin explores the visual dialogue between exaggerated mass and slender lines. By embracing contrast in thickness and volume as our primary design language, we challenge conventional furniture norms to craft sculptural pieces that command presence. We create objects that do not merely occupy space, but actively become the main character within it—bold, ideal, and experimental.

01
02

This philosophy runs across every category we touch, from shelving and lighting to tables, chairs, and full seating sets. Rather than starting from function and adding form as an afterthought, we let the sculptural gesture come first and invite function to follow—storage tucked into a curve, a seat that grows out of a plant's base, legs that borrow their silhouette from something entirely unrelated to furniture.
